# 1. How to Use This Log

This log records every decision made about FindHouseQuick and every point still open. It is read before any new piece of work starts. A decision recorded here may only be changed with the approval of the Managing Director, and any change is added as a new entry with its date.

Country managers may switch features off or pause them in their own countries at once; every other change they make (switching on, scheduling, gradual release, withdrawing a rule) needs a super admin’s approval. Switching a critical feature on always needs a second person, one of them a super admin. | 22 Oct 2026       |
| 29      | Staff two-step login  | Staff sign in with two steps. | 30      | Staff accounts        | Super admins add staff, set their roles and countries, and deactivate them through the admin endpoints, with every change recorded. The first super admin is created with a one-time server command.                                                                                                                                                                                     | 22 Oct 2026       |
| 31      | Staff two-step login  | ADR 0005 details confirmed: after 5 wrong second-step codes, the second step pauses for 15 minutes for that staff member. Two-step login for ordinary users is left for a later step.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | 22 Oct 2026       |
| 32      | Staff two-step login  | The staff second step lasts 3 hours, then must be done again. The period is a platform setting that super admins can change through the admin endpoints, with every change recorded.                                                                                                                                                                                                     | 22 Oct 2026       |
| 33      | Feature controls      | "Switching off at once" (decision 28) means an off rule with no start time, end time or percentage. An off rule with a start or end time made by a country manager waits for a super admin.                                                                                                                                                                                              | 22 Oct 2026       |
| 34      | Staff two-step login  | The second step must be a different factor from the sign-in. The platform records how each device signed in. A device signed in with an SMS code must complete the second step with an authenticator app code; a device signed in with a password may use an SMS code or an authenticator app code.                                                                                      | 22 Oct 2026       |
